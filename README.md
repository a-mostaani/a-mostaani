### Hi, I'm Arsham Mostaani

Applied ML researcher-engineer. I take reinforcement learning, bandits and optimization from research into production, and I study **task-oriented communication**: deciding what agents should say when bandwidth is scarce, judged by whether the task gets done.

**What I work on**

- **Online decision-making in production:** bandit-based operator selection for a live ride-pooling routing engine (ALNS): 3.6x faster convergence to 90% of the best-known solution.
- **Task-oriented compression for multi-agent RL:** my Ph.D. topic (University of Luxembourg), now ported to JAX with a value-certification layer.
- **LLM and voice agents:** voice agents and the harnesses that test them; next, how well voice agents complete tasks over noisy network channels.
